# JobAfriq Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25/11/2026

This policy explains what information the JobAfriq Android app ("JobAfriq", "we", "us") handles, and why. In short: **you don't create an account, and we don't collect your personal data.** The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to serve those ads, as explained below.

## 1. What JobAfriq is

JobAfriq shows job adverts that were already published publicly on other websites. It adds search, filters, job alerts, scam warnings and short summaries. Each job links to its original source. We are not the employer, we are not a recruitment agency, and we are not affiliated with the websites we link to. JobAfriq never charges job seekers.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us:

- **Saved (bookmarked) jobs**
- **Search filters** you chose (locations, categories, job type, experience, date posted, sort order, the "hide possible scams" setting)
- **Job alert settings**: locations, categories and keywords, plus a list of job IDs already checked, so you aren't notified twice
- **App settings**: theme, whether you accepted the first-launch notice, how many times the app has been opened, and when an ad was last shown (used to limit how often ads appear)
- **A cached copy of the job list**, so the app works offline

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this.

## 3. Information we collect

**None.** JobAfriq has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK and no server of our own. The job list is downloaded as static files from GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may log technical request data such as IP address and time, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

JobAfriq is free because it shows ads from **Google AdMob**. To serve, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's **advertising ID** (Android Advertising ID), or Android Privacy Sandbox signals,
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
- ad interactions (views, taps) and diagnostic data.

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Your choices**

- **Consent:** in regions where it is required (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete it in Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads** (or **Google → Ads**).
- **Fewer ads:** if offered, you can watch an optional ad in Settings to hide ads for 24 hours.

## 5. Notifications

If you turn on job alerts, Android asks you for permission to show notifications. The app then checks the public job list every few hours in the background, and compares it with your alert settings **on your device**. Your alert settings are never uploaded.

## 6. Links to other websites and apps

"View original post", apply links, email addresses and phone numbers open in your browser, email app or dialer. Those websites and services have their own privacy policies. Never share bank details, your BVN or NIN, or pay any fee to get a job. Genuine employers never ask you to pay for a job.

## 7. Children

JobAfriq is meant for job seekers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 8. Security

Data is downloaded over HTTPS. Information stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

## 10. Contact
